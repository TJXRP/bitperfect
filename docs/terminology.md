# Terminology

## Entropography

The protocol family and field name.

## Entropograph

The public stored artifact made from Entropographic Particles and public metadata.

## Entropographic Particles

The public particle components produced by the shatter path. In the v4 challenge, each particle decodes to 128 bytes.

## Private Anchors

Private paired material required for authorized reconstruction. Private anchors are not part of the attacker package.

## Authorization Material

Secret material required by the rejoin path. Authorization material is not part of the attacker package.
