Entropic Dispersal and Reassembly™ (EDR)

A Distributed Trust Data Protocol. No single breach reveals your data.

EDR splits a record into 1,024 masked particles across separate protected domains. Reconstruction requires the particle store, private anchors, authorization, and recovery nonce material. Breach all but one domain and get noise.

At reconstruction, everything converges in RAM only. That moment still has to be protected.
