Entropic Dispersal and Reassembly™ (EDR) v2.1

A local, exact-or-nothing data protection protocol. No single piece reveals your data.

EDR encrypts a record, then splits it into 1,024 particles. Every particle and the matching anchor are required to get anything back, and the result is the exact original or nothing. The key never leaves this browser tab.

At reconstruction, everything converges in RAM only. That moment still has to be protected.
